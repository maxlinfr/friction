# Friction: 2-week experiment

Thanks for helping test this. For two weeks, Instagram in Safari will **sometimes** get slowly
more annoying the longer you scroll Reels and the feed: slower loading, buffering, duller colours.
Each day is randomly an **ON** day (friction) or an **OFF** day (normal Instagram), so we can
compare how much you scroll on each.

Takes about 5 minutes to set up. Free, and nothing leaves your phone unless you send it.

## Setup (iPhone)

1. **Install "Userscripts"** (free, open source) from the App Store, and open it once.
2. **Turn the extension on:** Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → **Userscripts** → On.
   Under permissions, allow it on **instagram.com** (or "All Websites").
   *(On older iOS: Settings → Safari → Extensions.)*
3. **Install Friction:** open this link **in Safari**:
   `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxlinfr/friction/main/testing/friction-friends.user.js`
   Tap the **puzzle / aA icon** in the address bar → **Userscripts** → **Install**.
4. **Open instagram.com in Safari** and log in. Bookmark it, or keep the tab open.
   ⚠️ Don't use "Add to Home Screen": Safari extensions don't run in home-screen web apps.

## The one rule

**For the two weeks, use Instagram in Safari only, on ON and OFF days alike.**
If you open the real app, the experiment can't see that scrolling. Easiest: offload the
Instagram app (Settings → Apps → Instagram → Offload App: keeps your data, removes the app).
DMs, posting and stories all work on the website.

## Checking your numbers

Go to **instagram.com/#friction** to see your stats and whether today is ON or OFF.

At the end of the two weeks, open that page, tap **Copy results**, and send the code to Max.
The code only contains, per session: the date, ON/OFF, seconds spent on Reels/feed, and the
number of reels watched. No usernames, no content, nothing about who you follow or message.

## If something's off

- Reels never load, or stay blank: turn Userscripts off for instagram.com and tell Max.
- To quit: Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Userscripts → Off.
